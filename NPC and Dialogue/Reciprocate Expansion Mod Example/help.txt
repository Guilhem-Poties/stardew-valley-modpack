This is a template to make your mod support Reciprocate.
The most important thing is that your mod folder must have a folder named "Reciprocate."
With this setup, the Reciprocate mod will be able to detect your added content during operation.

Editing the files under Reciprocatei/i18n is for adding dialogues, following the same rules as the original version for supporting multiple languages.

Reciprocatei/GiftData/GiftPresets is for setting up gift data.
Reciprocatei/GiftData/NPCs contains NPC data, where NPCs will use the preset gift IDs to query which items they can give.
Reciprocatei/GiftData/IgnoreItems is a list of gifts that will not trigger Reciprocate, such as when your mod has items like movie tickets that don't require a return gift.
